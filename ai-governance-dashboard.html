Unacceptable Risk — Prohibited BANNED

AI systems that pose a clear threat to safety, livelihoods, and rights. Outright banned under the EU AI Act with no path to compliance.

Social scoring by govts Real-time biometric surveillance Subliminal manipulation Emotion recognition (workplace)
High Risk — Strict Controls Required REGULATED

Significant risk to health, safety, or fundamental rights. Requires conformity assessment, risk management system, human oversight, and registration in EU database.

CV screening / hiring AI Credit scoring Medical diagnostics Law enforcement AI Critical infrastructure
Limited Risk — Transparency Required DISCLOSURE

AI systems that interact with humans must disclose they are AI. Chatbots, deepfakes, and emotion recognition systems must notify users.

Chatbots / virtual assistants Deepfake content AI-generated text Spam filters
Minimal Risk — Voluntary Code of Conduct LOW RISK

The vast majority of AI systems. No mandatory requirements but voluntary adherence to codes of conduct is encouraged. Free to deploy with standard due diligence.

AI video games Spam filters AI-powered search Recommendation engines
NIST AI Risk Management Framework — Core Functions
Function 01
🏛️
GOVERN
Establish policies, accountability structures, and culture for AI risk management across the organization.
Define AI risk appetite & tolerance
Assign roles & accountability
Create AI use policies & standards
Establish workforce AI literacy
Engage stakeholders on AI risks
Function 02
🗺️
MAP
Categorize AI systems and identify the context, risks, and potential impacts before deployment.
Inventory all AI systems in use
Identify affected populations
Classify AI risk categories
Document data lineage & sources
Assess bias & fairness risks
Function 03
📊
MEASURE
Analyze, assess, and track AI risks using quantitative and qualitative tools and metrics.
Evaluate model performance & drift
Test for bias & adverse outcomes
Assess explainability & transparency
Review third-party AI vendor risk
Benchmark against industry standards
Function 04
⚙️
MANAGE
Prioritize and address AI risks through response plans, ongoing monitoring, and continuous improvement.
Implement risk treatment plans
Monitor deployed AI continuously
Manage AI incidents & responses
Decommission high-risk systems
Feed learnings back to GOVERN
